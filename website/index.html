Private by design

Your habits are none of our business.

Most apps require your mail, a subscription and still collect your data. Habitt doesn't. Open the app and start tracking right away.

Account

No email, no password, no sign-up wall. You are ready to go the second you open the app.

Ads

Nothing between you and your app interactions. No banners, no "watch a video to unlock".

Tracking

No analytics SDKs and no data collection. We don't know anything about you.

Backups that only you can read.

Still want to backup your habits and sync across devices? Turn on backup and Habitt encrypts everything on your phone before it goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud. Prefer local files? Export a backup to your device and import on another.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption, done on your device
  • Optional PIN to protect the encryption key
  • Sync across your phones through your own cloud

FAQ

Frequent questions.

Is Habitt really free?
Yes. Every feature is free and there's no limit on habits. There's an optional subscription, but it's a tip to support development and doesn't unlock anything.
Where is my data stored?
On your phone. Habitt has no servers that store habit data. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud, and only your device holds the key. Read the full privacy policy.
Do I need an account?
No. Open the app and start. You only sign in with Google if you choose Google Drive backup, and that sign-in is between you and Google.
What happens if I change phones?
Turn on Google Drive or iCloud backup, or export a local backup file, then restore it on the new phone. Your whole history comes along.
Can I import from another habit app?
You can import from HabitKit today, including your full completion history. You can merge it with existing habits or replace everything.
Does it work without internet?
Completely. Internet is only used for optional cloud backup.
Which platforms are supported?
iPhone (iOS 15.6 or later) and Android. Habitt is available on the App Store and Google Play.
