## Description
*(2331 chars)*

Habitt is a habit tracker built around one idea: your data belongs to you.
No account required. No internet needed. No analytics, no ads.

TRACK YOUR WAY
Choose how each habit is measured: by count (glasses of water, pages read, reps) or by time (minutes of exercise, hours of study). Set a goal that fits your life, and raise the bar as you improve.

Scheduling is flexible so your habits follow your rhythm:
• Daily, weekly, monthly, or custom intervals
• Specific days of the week or month
• Organise by time of day: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Any Time

STAY MOTIVATED
Habitt tracks your streak and all-time longest streak for every habit. But it isn't harsh about it.

Habit Strength monitors your recent consistency and acts like a personal coach. If you miss a few days it'll tell you that your target might be too ambitious and suggest lowering it down. If you're crushing it, it'll push you to increase your target. It meets you where you are, not where you think you should be.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Per-habit heatmap and completion calendar
• Streak history and statistics
• Strength score with trend insights

REMINDERS
Add multiple notifications to any habit. Configure them exactly when you need them and on which days a week - no more forgetting.

BACKUP
Habitt stores everything on your device. When you want a cloud copy, connect your Google account and the app automatically syncs an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. Data is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it is ever transferred. We cannot read it, neither can anyone else.

QUICK START
Import all your habits from other habit tracking apps such as HabitKit. Use a premade template to get going in seconds: wake up early, go to bed early, gym, running, walking, drink water, brush teeth, skin care, nutrition, studying, reading, medications, productivity sessions, and more.

APPEARANCE
Dark and light mode.

MADE BY ONE DEVELOPER
Habitt is built and maintained by a single developer. Every feature is available with no limits on how many habits you can track. Subscriptions support the app's continued development — every subscriber genuinely makes a difference.

Feedback, ideas, or bug reports are welcome. Leave a review or get in touch — it helps more than you think.
