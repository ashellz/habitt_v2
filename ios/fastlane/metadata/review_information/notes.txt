A description of the app's purpose and target audience, including the problem it solves and the value it provides: It's a privacy-first habit tracking app that helps users build and stick to daily routines without giving up their data, restricting everything with paywalls or show ads. Unlike most of the competition that need accounts and collect user behavior and data, Habitt stores everything locally. No sign-up, no analytics, no servers needed. You can track habits by count or time spent, set flexible schedules (daily, weekly, monthly, or custom - every X days), and organize them by time of day. Built-in streaks, progress charts, a habit strength score, and personalized coaching messages keep users motivated as they grow. If they want to back up or sync across devices, they can opt in and connect their own Google Drive — but data is encrypted on their phone before it goes to their gdrive, so even we intercept can't read it nor can google. With premade templates for things like hydration, sleep, workouts, reading, and medications, its easy for users to start. Available in English, German, Spanish, Italian, and Bosnian. It's is built for anyone who wants a simple, honest ap that respects their privacy and helps them show up for themselves every day and build real habits that stick.
